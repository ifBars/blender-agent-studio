# Experimental systematic whole-scene workflow

Treat the scene as a set of reusable assets connected by explicit layout and
support relationships. Record an asset ledger with each required family,
instance count, budget, intended features, support, evidence and current state.
Keep a durable work_state.md so unfinished or weak families cannot disappear
from attention as the scene grows.

First block out the whole layout and player route at real scale. Validate
building orientation, functional zones, approach widths and support heights.
Use a shared coordinate system and derive placements from named anchors.

Then build one representative of each family, in dependency order: foundations
and architecture, platforms and furniture, cargo and utility props, vegetation
and accents. Work in small batches. For each representative, inspect its full
silhouette, reverse side, relevant joints, geometry budget and material export.
Do not replicate an unfinished asset across the scene. Preserve intended
low-poly facets; secondary detail should clarify construction rather than add
unnecessary density. Place approved instances with deliberate rotation and
variation, checking their actual support surfaces.

After each batch, revisit the whole scene from player height and overhead.
Check access, relative scale, contact, unintended intersections and consistency
with earlier assets. Inspect actual geometry for uncertain relationships;
SceneIR bounding boxes only narrow the investigation. Record what is measured,
what is visible and what is still unclear. Repair the causal parameters while
preserving already accepted assets.

Reserve the last part of the budget for integration and export. Review every
family in close and reverse views, including background props. A strong depot
or one attractive screenshot must not hide incomplete furniture or vegetation.
Reimport the GLB, verify layout and material preservation, reproduce the source,
and list every unresolved per-asset or scene-wide issue. Never mark a ledger row
complete merely because its object exists or its triangle budget passes.
